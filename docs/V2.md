# Version 2 Architecture & Features

## Reservation workflow

Members can reserve unavailable books. A reservation is linked to both the member and book and has a lifecycle:

`pending → fulfilled` or `pending → cancelled`

## Notification engine

The dashboard triggers notification generation for:
- 3 days before due date
- 1 day before due date
- due today
- overdue items

This is intentionally implemented as a service so it can later be moved into a scheduled background worker such as Celery or APScheduler.

## Analytics

The analytics service computes:
- Most circulated books
- Circulation by category over the last 30 days
- Pending reservations
- New members over the last 30 days

## Fine payments

`FinePayment` separates a calculated circulation fine from an actual payment transaction. This allows future support for partial payments, payment gateways, receipts and reconciliation.
